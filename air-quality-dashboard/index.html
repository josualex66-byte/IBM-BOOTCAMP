About the Data

Source
Global Air Pollution Dataset — Kaggle. Snapshot dataset; no temporal dimension.
AQI Definition
Air Quality Index (AQI) converts pollutant concentrations into a 0–500 scale. The overall AQI equals the highest sub-index among CO, Ozone, NO2, and PM2.5.
Categories
Good (0–50) Moderate (51–100) Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (101–150) Unhealthy (151–200) Very Unhealthy (201–300) Hazardous (301–500)
Limitations
No timestamps — trends cannot be inferred. No coordinates — city-level map pins are not available. 427 rows have no country assigned (labelled "Unknown" and excluded from country-level charts). Rankings use a minimum threshold of 5 cities per country.
Data cleaning
1 row with no city was removed. Country names were normalised to ISO-3 codes for the choropleth map. AQI categories were standardised to a fixed order.
